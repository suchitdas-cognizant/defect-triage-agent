# Member 5 Prompt: Demo And Reports Owner

## Role

You own sample outputs, demo script, and presentation readiness.

## Prompt To Run

You are the Demo and Reports Owner for this Defect Triaging Agent PoC.

Your goal is to make the PoC easy to demonstrate in three minutes. Read:

- `docs/demo-script.md`
- `reports/`
- `data/sample-defects.json`
- `prompts/defect-triage-prompt.md`
- `templates/triage-report-template.md`

Then polish the demo and sample outputs.

Deliverables:

1. Improve `docs/demo-script.md` so the demo is clear and under three minutes.
2. Ensure at least three reports exist in `reports/`.
3. Make each report issue-comment-ready.
4. Add a short `docs/demo-runbook.md` with exact demo steps.
5. Prepare a final spoken summary for the closing slide or narration.

Acceptance criteria:

- The demo can be delivered without live debugging.
- The sample reports are consistent and professional.
- The demo clearly shows repeatability across multiple defects.
- The closing explains business impact and no-infrastructure advantage.

Time box: 90 minutes.
